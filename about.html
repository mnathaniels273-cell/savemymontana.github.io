About Save My Montana Initiative

ODIMUS Green Legacy Stewardship / Omni‑Aid Foundation

Who We Are

Save My Montana is a grassroots, Montana‑born initiative focused on regenerative land care, in‑home wellness support, telehealth connectivity, and youth empowerment. We serve rural and Tribal communities with practical, frontier‑ready tools that help people access care where they live — not miles away.

What We Do

We design and deploy systems that support Medicare/Medicaid dual‑eligible Montanans, elders aging in place, people with disabilities, behavioral‑health needs, and families facing social drivers of health challenges.

  • In‑home MedBay micro‑clinics
  • Portable wellness pods
  • Telehealth connectivity (Starlink‑enabled)
  • AI‑supported triage tools
  • Youth workforce training
  • Environmental and water restoration
  • Family support systems

Our Commitment

We do not provide medical treatment. We provide the technology, infrastructure, and support systems that help licensed clinicians reach rural homes safely and effectively.
